Growth drivers. Three structural step-changes drive the curve from Q2 2026 onwards. (1) Delegated Authority closed June 2026 lifts net economics on the embedded book from ~45% to 65%+, increasing Frankly's revenue per policy by ~44% on the existing book before any volume growth. (2) Classical suite launched July 2026 adds five classical lines (home contents, car, pet, liability/PA, travel) with materially higher per-policyholder GWP than embedded device protection, driving GWP per policyholder from ~€86/yr to ~€491+/yr. (3) Embedded partner pipeline (Soundboks, Bike&Co, Daells Bolighus, Hi-Fi Klubben, AndLight) ramps from H2 2026, with Soundboks alone targeting 150.000-200.000 new policies annually. The decelerating growth rates from Q1 2027 onwards reflect the natural maturation of these compounding effects as the base grows.
